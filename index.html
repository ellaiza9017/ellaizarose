LuminangVoices of the Regions · a language-learning mobile game

● trailer

A growing silence is erasing the voices of the Philippine archipelago. Amira has to wake each region’s language before it disappears.

Following her grandfather’s final message, Amira travels across Cebu, Ilocos and Lanao to recover the Language Crystals, symbols of culture, memory and identity, before the Eclipse of Silence arrives.

Android Low-poly 3D Unity Blender Team of 4

3D Artist & Asset Designer · Unity co-developer
